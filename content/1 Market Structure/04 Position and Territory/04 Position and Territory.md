# Position and Territory

This chapter settles two linked decisions: how buyers should understand the offer and what will keep the position defensible after the market attracts competitors. Position chooses the comparison in the buyer's mind. Territory accumulates a reason the customer can feel for staying and a reason a rival can't quickly copy.

The work begins before a named rival appears. A category entry point determines when the buyer remembers you. Competitive geography reveals the alternatives that already win deals, including delay and an internal build. A fast follower test then asks what you've accumulated while the market was still yours to teach.

## From Contrast to Structural Hold

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[MS-022 Positioning as Contrast]] | Choose the alternative that makes your strongest difference decisive. |
| [[MS-023 Category Creation]] | Install a shared category definition and the institutions that keep it legible. |
| [[MS-024 Category Entry Point]] | Attach the category to the situations that bring a buyer into the market. |
| [[MS-025 Competitive Geography]] | Map what buyers actually choose when you lose, including delay and non-consumption. |
| [[MS-026 Fast Follower Arrives]] | Test what a better-funded entrant would need time to acquire before it could take your customers. |
| [[MS-027 Economic Moat]] | Build a structural hold that a copied feature or larger campaign can't erase. |
| [[MS-028 Data Gravity Lock-In]] | Deepen a hold through customer data and integrations that create continuing value. |
| [[MS-029 Trusted Neutral]] | Occupy common ground through governance that interested competitors can't credibly own. |

The order loops. Win-loss evidence can force a new contrast. A new entry point can expose a category boundary. A follower can reveal that an assumed moat was only a temporary product lead.

## Keep Position and Territory Separate

[[MS-022 Positioning as Contrast]] chooses the alternative. [[CR-027 Differentiation Axis]] later chooses the dimension on which the comparison runs. You can compare against an internal build and ask buyers to judge the choice by time to deployment; the two choices remain separable.

[[MS-005 Category Before Product]] owns the buyer's initial filing decision. [[MS-023 Category Creation]] owns the sustained program that makes a new category legible across the market. [[MS-024 Category Entry Point]] owns the trigger that brings the category to mind.

[[MS-027 Economic Moat]] owns the general test for structural durability. [[MS-028 Data Gravity Lock-In]] is a specific hold created by accumulated customer investment. [[MS-029 Trusted Neutral]] depends on credible governance. [[GTM-014 Brand as Moat]] owns the trust that compounds around a name.

[[MS-025 Competitive Geography]] maps the field. Positioning chooses which part of that field should organize the buyer's judgment.

## Handoff to Focus

[[05 Sizing and Focus]] inherits a claimed position and an account of why it can hold. It asks where that claim can become dense enough to produce references and favorable economics. It also tests whether those wins open an adjacent segment. Territory without a first segment remains an intention; focus gives it a place to accumulate.
