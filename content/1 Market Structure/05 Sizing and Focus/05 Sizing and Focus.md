# Sizing and Focus

A market claim has to resolve into an ordered set of customers you can win. This chapter settles where the first concentration will occur and how that concentration can open the next one. A credible market-size number records that sequence. Its strength comes from the route between specific wins.

Focus has several levels. Segmentation identifies groups that buy differently. A beachhead selects one group to win. Spear fishing names the account-level response when a small number of companies carry most of the value. A refusal policy protects those decisions once near-term revenue begins pulling the company elsewhere.

## From Market Story to First Wins

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[MS-030 Sizing Is Storytelling]] | Build the market estimate from a priced buyer and a credible expansion sequence. |
| [[MS-031 Market Segmentation]] | Divide buyers only where behavior calls for different commercial treatment. |
| [[MS-032 Beachhead Market]] | Choose one narrow segment whose complete win can produce proof for the adjacent segment. |
| [[MS-033 Spear Fishing]] | Pursue named accounts individually when revenue and reference value are highly concentrated. |
| [[MS-034 No Is the Most Profitable Word]] | Protect the chosen focus with written conditions for refusal. |

The order exposes weak plans early. A bottom-up size with no reachable first segment is a forecast without a route. A beachhead whose success doesn't transfer is a niche with no expansion logic. A named-account plan applied to a diffuse market spends expensive attention where a repeatable channel would work better.

## From Whole Market to Named Account

[[MS-030 Sizing Is Storytelling]] describes the whole opportunity and the path through it. [[MS-031 Market Segmentation]] identifies groups that require different treatment. [[MS-032 Beachhead Market]] chooses the first group to dominate. [[MS-033 Spear Fishing]] chooses the pursuit method when the decisive group can be reduced to a short account list.

An ICP and a beachhead also sit at different levels. [[MS-010 ICP Definition]] names the company-person fit that deserves the offer. A beachhead is the first market concentration where enough of those fits can produce shared proof.

[[MS-034 No Is the Most Profitable Word]] owns the standing decision that keeps both choices intact. It becomes useful before an off-strategy deal arrives, while the company can still define its exceptions without quota pressure in the room.

## Handoff to Economic Proof

[[06 Market and Money]] inherits a bottom-up market story and a focused route through it. It tests whether the financing plan supports that route and whether the customers won through it produce viable unit economics. Focus becomes evidence when those economics reflect the segment you said you could own.
