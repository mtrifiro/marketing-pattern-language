# The Primary Motion

Part I supplies the deal economics and buying structure that constrain go-to-market design. This chapter lets those facts select the motion that leads. Several channels can contribute to revenue, but one motion has to set the product experience and the economics around it.

The decision is architectural. Self-serve and human-carried deals ask the company to build different systems. Within a human-led motion, the founder's early role also differs from the field team that follows. Combining motions works when one remains primary and the other enters under a defined condition.

## From Motion Choice to Repetition

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[GTM-001 Single Primary Motion]] | Choose the leading motion from deal economics and buying structure, then make the other functions answer to it. |
| [[GTM-002 PLG-SLG Hybrid]] | Add human sales beneath a product-led motion when account value or purchase risk exceeds what self-service can carry. |
| [[GTM-003 Founder-Led Sales]] | Keep the founder in the sale while the story and buyer response are still changing deal by deal. |
| [[GTM-004 Repeatable Sales Motion]] | Extract the recurring path from completed deals and give a new seller a system they can run without the founder. |

These patterns cover two related paths. Product-led companies may add a sales layer once usage reveals larger accounts. Human-led companies move from founder judgment to a documented motion. Both paths require evidence that the next layer can repay its cost.

## Where the Hybrid Boundaries Sit

A motion is the system by which a buyer reaches value and a purchase. A channel is a route through which that system reaches the buyer. [[11 Channels and Ecosystem]] owns those outside routes after the primary motion is clear.

[[GTM-002 PLG-SLG Hybrid]] owns the architecture of a combined product-led and sales-led system. [[GTM-027 Product-Qualified Lead]] later owns the behavior that qualifies a person or account. [[GTM-028 Sales-Assisted Conversion]] owns the human response once that signal appears.

[[GTM-003 Founder-Led Sales]] and [[GTM-004 Repeatable Sales Motion]] describe successive stages. The founder discovers and revises the motion. Repeatability begins when another seller can close the same kind of deal through explicit criteria and shared material.

## Handoff to First Contact

[[08 First Contact]] inherits a primary motion and the role that enters first. It can decide what that person may experience before paying, then set the preparation required to reach initial value. A field-led motion may still give a practitioner useful access before a live conversation; a product-led motion depends on that access carrying more of the sale.
