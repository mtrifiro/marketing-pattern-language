# The Demand Engine

Demand work has to settle two questions before it buys reach: whether the market needs education or contains intent ready to capture, and whose credibility can carry the message. The answer depends on the market type chosen in [[MS-002 Market Type Sets the Playbook]]. A new market needs language and trusted interpreters before search volume or a familiar buying process can exist.

This chapter follows demand from creation to account-level action. It begins with the spending order, moves through the people and material that carry trust, and ends with the signals and shared account focus that turn otherwise private interest into coordinated work.

## From Market Education to Account Action

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[GTM-008 Demand Creation vs. Demand Capture]] | Fund education first where intent has to be created, then size capture to the intent that appears. |
| [[GTM-009 Awareness Cascade]] | Seed the story with credible insiders and let trust move through connected buyer groups. |
| [[GTM-010 Technical Content Marketing]] | Publish material that solves a real practitioner problem and can withstand expert scrutiny. |
| [[GTM-011 Content as Lead Filter]] | Use depth and buyer-specific language to attract people whose engagement carries commercial meaning. |
| [[GTM-012 Targeted Reputation Building]] | Define the finite set of people whose trust would change access to the market. |
| [[GTM-013 Authority Amplification]] | Give credible operators a sustained public voice and distribute authority across more than one person. |
| [[GTM-014 Brand as Moat]] | Protect the trust that accumulates around the company after repeated promises hold. |
| [[GTM-015 Dark Funnel Illumination]] | Aggregate private and product-adjacent signals into account-level evidence without pretending every influence is attributable. |
| [[GTM-016 Account-Based Everything]] | Align the company around the same named accounts when a short list decides the outcome. |

## Where Demand Work Splits

[[MS-002 Market Type Sets the Playbook]] owns the type and the first-cycle objective. [[GTM-008 Demand Creation vs. Demand Capture]] owns the order of spending. [[GTM-009 Awareness Cascade]] owns one mechanism by which a trusted interpretation spreads.

[[GTM-010 Technical Content Marketing]] owns the substance required by a skeptical practitioner. [[GTM-011 Content as Lead Filter]] owns the qualifying effect of that substance. Useful technical material can filter strongly even when it stays ungated.

[[GTM-012 Targeted Reputation Building]] begins with the people whose trust matters. [[GTM-013 Authority Amplification]] develops the credible operators who can earn that trust. [[GTM-014 Brand as Moat]] owns the company-level standing that accumulates after promises keep holding.

[[GTM-015 Dark Funnel Illumination]] detects account activity that ordinary attribution misses. [[GTM-016 Account-Based Everything]] decides how the company acts once account concentration calls for one shared list.
