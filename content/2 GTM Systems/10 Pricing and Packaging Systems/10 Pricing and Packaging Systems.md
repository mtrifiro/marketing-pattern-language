# Pricing and Packaging Systems

Chapter 3 chose the buyer-selecting price and its value unit. It also set the margin the market permits. This chapter turns those decisions into a revenue system a customer can enter and a team can operate. It settles the billing model and offer structure.

The work has an order. The billing model governs how revenue changes with customer behavior. The edition ladder gives distinct buyers a usable choice. Gates create movement between editions. Bundles group products a buyer already treats as one job.

## From Meter to Offer

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[GTM-017 Usage-Based Pricing]] | Bill consumption of the chosen value unit when customer success and usage can rise together. |
| [[GTM-018 Good-Better-Best]] | Build a short vertical ladder in which each edition fits a distinct level of buyer need. |
| [[GTM-019 Gating Line]] | Put the paid boundary after meaningful adoption and at a point where success creates willingness to upgrade. |
| [[GTM-020 Bundle Logic]] | Group capabilities horizontally when buyers already understand them as one purchase; keep specialist value available as an add-on. |

Usage-based pricing is conditional. It works when the meter tracks delivered value closely enough that a rising bill remains legible to the customer. Another billing model can fit better when that relationship is clearer.

## Where Packaging Decisions Split

[[MS-019 Unit of Value]] chooses the dimension along which value should be charged. [[GTM-017 Usage-Based Pricing]] owns how consumption of that dimension is billed and how the bill expands. The unit is a market decision; the model is the operating machinery built on it.

[[GTM-018 Good-Better-Best]] builds a vertical path from one edition to the next. [[GTM-020 Bundle Logic]] combines modules across a horizontal line when the buyer sees one job. A tier answers how much of the product a buyer needs; a bundle answers which products belong in one purchase.

[[GTM-006 Generous Free Tier]] owns the complete free experience. [[GTM-019 Gating Line]] owns the point where that experience creates a justified paid boundary. [[CR-030 Pricing Page Transparency]] later decides what showing or concealing the resulting price communicates.

## Handoff to Channels

[[11 Channels and Ecosystem]] inherits a saleable offer. Partners need enough margin and clarity to carry it. Marketplaces need a package that fits an existing procurement route. APIs need a billing model that third-party use won't make surprising or punitive.
