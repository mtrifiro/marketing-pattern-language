# Channels and Ecosystem

A primary motion reaches farther when people and systems outside the company can carry it. That extension also transfers control to whoever operates the route. Partners choose which offer receives attention, while marketplaces set transaction rules. An external developer can still walk away from an API that isn't worth building on. This chapter settles which outside routes fit the motion and what has to be proven before each route can work.

The sequence begins with the practitioner because technical markets often enter through use and peer trust. Advocacy gives that practitioner a credible human counterpart. An API lets outside builders extend the product. Partners and marketplaces add commercial reach once demand and a direct motion already exist.

## From Practitioner Trust to Outside Reach

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[GTM-021 Practitioner-First GTM]] | Enter through the person who can experience technical value and equip them to reach the purchaser. |
| [[GTM-022 Developer Advocacy]] | Give practitioners a credible peer whose standing is protected from direct sales pressure. |
| [[GTM-023 Public API Flywheel]] | Let external developers extend the product through a documented interface that becomes easier to adopt as integrations accumulate. |
| [[GTM-024 Partner Force Multipliers]] | Add resellers or service partners after the direct motion can show repeatable demand and reference customers. |
| [[GTM-025 Marketplace Presence]] | Remove procurement friction where buyers already hold approved spend, after demand points them toward the listing. |

The order can branch. A public API and developer advocacy may develop together. A marketplace can precede a broad partner program when cloud procurement is already central to the buyer. The invariant is that outside reach extends a working motion; it needs something proven to carry.

## What Outside Routes Actually Do

[[GTM-024 Partner Force Multipliers]] gives another company a role in selling or delivering the offer. [[GTM-025 Marketplace Presence]] gives the buyer an established transaction route. A partner may use a marketplace, but the commercial contribution and the procurement mechanism remain separate.

[[GTM-023 Public API Flywheel]] is a product surface through which others build. [[GTM-022 Developer Advocacy]] is a human function that earns trust among practitioners. One expands capability through code; the other builds understanding through peer credibility.

[[GTM-021 Practitioner-First GTM]] is a market posture. It can support a free product-led entry or a paid technical sale. The defining choice is that practitioner value and trust come before the purchaser's commercial case, with a planned bridge between them.

## Handoff to the Growth Loop

[[12 The Growth Loop]] inherits accounts brought in through direct and outside routes. It reads product behavior to decide when a person should intervene. The post-sale system then determines whether those accounts expand. Channel reach becomes durable only when the customers it brings strengthen the next cycle.
