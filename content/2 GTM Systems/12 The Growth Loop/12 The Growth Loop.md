# The Growth Loop

The first sale is one transition inside a longer system. This chapter settles how product use becomes a commercial signal and how a person responds. It then gives the installed base enough ownership and investment to grow. The loop closes when retained customers expand and help the next round of acquisition through proof or referral.

The patterns move from system design to capital allocation through explicit signal and ownership decisions.

## From Product Signal to Base-First Growth

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[GTM-026 Growth Loop]] | Define the closed circuit from acquisition through retention and back into the next cycle. |
| [[GTM-027 Product-Qualified Lead]] | Qualify people through realized product value and aggregate related users into an account signal. |
| [[GTM-028 Sales-Assisted Conversion]] | Send a specialist with account-usage context when a high-value or complex account needs help beyond self-service. |
| [[GTM-029 Land and Expand]] | Enter through a bounded use case and build explicit machinery for account growth after the foothold works. |
| [[GTM-030 Freemium-to-Enterprise]] | Consolidate a meaningful free footprint into one governed company purchase. |
| [[GTM-031 Support Is Sales]] | Treat support interactions as moments that affect retention and expansion. |
| [[GTM-032 Customer Success as Growth Engine]] | Give one revenue-owning function responsibility for the installed base. |
| [[GTM-033 Expansion Before Acquisition]] | Give the base first claim on growth investment and use net revenue retention to enforce the rule. |

## Who Owns Each Transition

[[GTM-002 PLG-SLG Hybrid]] decides whether product-led and sales-led work belong in one architecture and which motion leads. [[GTM-027 Product-Qualified Lead]] defines the signal. [[GTM-028 Sales-Assisted Conversion]] defines the intervention that follows.

[[GTM-029 Land and Expand]] owns the general account-growth path from a small foothold. [[GTM-030 Freemium-to-Enterprise]] is the specific consolidation sale in which the foothold consists of free users inside one company.

[[GTM-031 Support Is Sales]] governs a class of customer interactions. [[GTM-032 Customer Success as Growth Engine]] owns the function responsible for retention and expansion. [[GTM-033 Expansion Before Acquisition]] owns the investment order and the net revenue retention metric that holds it accountable.

These distinctions keep one team from becoming responsible for the whole loop by implication. Each transition needs a named owner. Its trigger and response must be observable by the next part of the system.

## Handoff to Operations

[[13 Sensemaking]] inherits evidence from a live system. Part III turns those signals into routines that can correct the motion. A loop compounds only while the company can detect when its assumptions have stopped matching the market.
