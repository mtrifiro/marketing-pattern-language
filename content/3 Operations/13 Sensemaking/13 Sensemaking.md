# Sensemaking

Part III begins with the operation that keeps every earlier choice open to correction. A market speaks through buyer language and observed outcomes across the live system. This chapter settles how the company turns those observations into a shared interpretation before it increases spend or automates the motion.

The sequence separates hearing from acting. Win-loss work preserves and tests the evidence. A feedback gate decides whether a motion has earned more scale. A closed loop gives the finding authority and returns an answer to the customer who supplied the signal.

## From Market Signal to Changed Decision

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[OPS-001 Marketing as Sensemaking]] | Make an accurate market read the operation's primary output and judge campaigns by what they teach as well as what they produce. |
| [[OPS-002 Win-Loss Discipline]] | Preserve buyer language and use independent completed-deal review to find recurring reasons. |
| [[OPS-003 Feedback Before Scale]] | Hold a motion at its current level until buyer fit and economic evidence support a larger commitment. |
| [[OPS-004 Closing the Loop]] | Route each material finding to a decision owner and tell the customer what changed. |

This order prevents scale from burying the evidence it was meant to test. It also prevents research from becoming an archive of findings that never reach the product or the go-to-market system.

## From Finding to Authority

[[OPS-001 Marketing as Sensemaking]] owns the operating purpose. [[OPS-002 Win-Loss Discipline]] supplies one recurring source of evidence and protects the buyer's own language from being reduced to a convenient dropdown reason.

[[OPS-003 Feedback Before Scale]] is a gate on a specific go-to-market assumption. [[OPS-019 Quarterly Recalibration]] later reopens the larger strategic choices on a fixed review rhythm. One controls when a motion receives more commitment; the other checks whether the company is still running the right motion.

[[OPS-002 Win-Loss Discipline]] ends with an interpreted finding. [[OPS-004 Closing the Loop]] begins when that finding needs authority. A decision owner must act on it and respond to the source.

## Handoff to the Engine Room

[[14 The Engine Room]] inherits a motion that has enough evidence to formalize. It defines stages and installs shared memory. The response systems then connect around that model. Automation becomes useful after sensemaking has established what the system should recognize and where it should send the signal.
