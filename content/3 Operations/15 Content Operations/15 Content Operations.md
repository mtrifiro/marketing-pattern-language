# Content Operations

Content becomes an operating asset when the company manages it as a maintained, reusable stock with a credible publishing promise. This chapter settles how a team manages that stock across ordinary quarters. Inspect the existing inventory before approving new production.

The four patterns give the system an accounting rule and a production rhythm. One establishes the inventory and maintenance obligation. The next turns a substantial source into planned derivatives. Editorial cadence limits the promise to what the team can sustain. Documentation receives its own discipline because a technical buyer often evaluates it while using the product.

## From Inventory to Maintained Use

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[OPS-012 Content as Capital Asset]] | Treat durable material as an inventoried asset with a maintenance owner. |
| [[OPS-013 Repurposing Pipeline]] | Plan the derivatives of a substantial source before publication so research can support more than one surface. |
| [[OPS-014 Editorial Cadence]] | Set a publishing promise a normal quarter can keep and protect it with prepared material. |
| [[OPS-015 Documentation First]] | Treat documentation as a maintained product surface that helps a technical buyer evaluate and use the offer. |

The sequence applies to a library, while each new anchor runs through it again. The inventory exposes a gap. The anchor supplies source material. Planned derivatives fill specific uses. Cadence determines when the team can publish and maintain them.

## What Content Operations Owns

[[GTM-011 Content as Lead Filter]] owns the qualifying purpose of buyer-specific material. [[GTM-010 Technical Content Marketing]] owns the standard of usefulness and technical depth. This chapter owns the operating system that keeps such work findable and current.

[[OPS-013 Repurposing Pipeline]] governs how one source becomes several assets. [[CR-020 Social Media Playbook]] later adapts an asset to a platform's native form. [[OPS-009 Messaging Cadence]] governs how often the audience hears the central claim. The source workflow stays separate from the surface form, and message timing remains its own decision.

[[OPS-015 Documentation First]] treats documentation as a continuing product and evaluation surface. It draws on technical-content standards, but it also has versioning and product-use obligations that an article doesn't carry.

## Handoff to Team and Rhythm

[[16 Team and Rhythm]] inherits a visible body of work and a sustainable promise. It decides when generalists should give way to specialists and how sales carries the narrative. A review rhythm keeps the system current. A content operation survives growth when those responsibilities remain explicit through each handoff.
