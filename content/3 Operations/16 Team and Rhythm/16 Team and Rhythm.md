# Team and Rhythm

Strategy changes when it has to survive outside the founder's head. This chapter settles how the team divides work. It also keeps the market story coherent across sellers and installs a fixed review rhythm for correcting drift. The goal is a system that preserves learning while more people carry it.

The patterns follow the company's growth. Generalists keep early feedback close to execution. Narrative reinforcement checks what sellers actually say. Enablement turns a proven approach into maintained material and practice. Recalibration reopens the plan on a schedule before accumulated drift becomes a missed quarter.

## From Early Team to Standing Review

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[OPS-016 Generalists Then Specialists]] | Keep early roles broad while the motion is changing; specialize when repeated volume makes a narrower role worth its handoffs. |
| [[OPS-017 Sales as Narrative Reinforcement]] | Inspect what sellers say and route recurring buyer response into the next message revision. |
| [[OPS-018 Sales Enablement Architecture]] | Maintain the playbooks and practice that let each seller carry the same proven motion. |
| [[OPS-019 Quarterly Recalibration]] | Use monthly reviews for operating decisions and a quarterly review to retest strategic assumptions. |

Specialization increases capacity and creates seams. The later patterns keep the market read connected to the words used in the field and the decisions made in review.

## What Changes as the Team Grows

[[GTM-003 Founder-Led Sales]] owns the period in which the founder discovers a sale and proves another person can carry it. [[OPS-016 Generalists Then Specialists]] owns the broader staffing rule for an operation whose work is still changing.

[[OPS-017 Sales as Narrative Reinforcement]] governs the live behavior of the sales floor and the feedback it returns. [[OPS-018 Sales Enablement Architecture]] governs the maintained system that prepares sellers for that behavior. A current battle card is enablement; evidence that buyers reject its premise belongs in narrative reinforcement and sensemaking.

[[OPS-019 Quarterly Recalibration]] contains two review altitudes. The monthly beat assigns decisions against current operations. The quarterly beat retests the assumptions that define those operations. Both require a pre-read and a named owner for each decision.

## Handoff to Measurement

[[17 Measurement]] inherits recurring decisions and the review rooms where they get made. It can ask which numbers would change those decisions and which indicators arrive early enough to help. It also identifies where direct inspection has to supplement a dashboard.
