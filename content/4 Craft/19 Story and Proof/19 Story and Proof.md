# Story and Proof

A buyer encounters the company's claim in pieces and over time. This chapter settles the order of those pieces and the evidence that lets the buyer keep moving. The story begins before a product evaluation and continues through the internal financial case. Each pattern owns a different part of that movement.

The buyer's journey is the organizing frame. Teaching makes an unfamiliar purchase possible. Original data and customer evidence give the claim weight. Proof has to appear beside the doubt it answers. A demo compresses the value story into a live experience, while the ROI narrative translates it into the buyer's own economics.

## From Journey Arc to Financial Case

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[CR-011 Buyer's Journey Narrative]] | Write the full arc from problem recognition to commitment and assign each asset to the question at its stage. |
| [[CR-012 Teach the Buyer to Buy]] | Give a new-category buyer the evaluation method and internal process required to make the purchase. |
| [[CR-013 Data-Driven Storytelling]] | Build the argument around original data or a bounded customer result with a stated baseline. |
| [[CR-014 Social Proof Architecture]] | Place the right kind of evidence beside the claim or hesitation it can answer. |
| [[CR-015 Demo Script]] | Script a live product encounter around a buyer situation and the moment the product resolves it. |
| [[CR-016 ROI Narrative]] | Build the return and payback case from the buyer's inputs so their finance team can reproduce it. |

The sequence changes with the purchase. A technical evaluator may meet the demo before the financial case. A category buyer may need teaching before they can interpret any proof. [[CR-011 Buyer's Journey Narrative]] keeps those local choices inside one coherent progression.

## Keep Arc and Evidence Separate

[[CR-011 Buyer's Journey Narrative]] owns the whole buyer progression. [[CR-012 Teach the Buyer to Buy]] owns the stretch where the buyer needs a method for evaluating and sponsoring an unfamiliar purchase.

[[CR-013 Data-Driven Storytelling]] builds a narrative around evidence the company or customer generated. [[CR-014 Social Proof Architecture]] decides where each piece of evidence belongs along the reading path. [[CR-016 ROI Narrative]] uses the buyer's own financial inputs for the part of the story that has to survive internal scrutiny.

[[CR-015 Demo Script]] is a live application of the value story. It can compress several journey stages into one encounter, but it still needs a specific buyer situation and the evidence appropriate to that point in the purchase.
