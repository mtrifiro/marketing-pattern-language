# Assets and Surfaces

A message meets different constraints on every surface. The homepage has to orient a stranger, while the one-pager has to travel through an internal buying group. A long-form recording asks the audience for sustained attention and has to repay it. This chapter settles how the chosen message and story survive those conditions.

The five patterns cover a visual rule and four applied surfaces. Visual hierarchy determines the reading order within any layout. The homepage frames a transformation at the company's front door. The one-pager compresses the case for a reader you may never meet. Social adapts the same story to a platform's native form. Long-form audio and video build authority through depth and continuity.

## Match the Surface to Its Job

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[CR-017 Visual Hierarchy]] | Rank what the eye should meet and spend contrast according to that order. |
| [[CR-018 Homepage Before-After-Why]] | Build the before-after transformation and attach one reason to believe it. |
| [[CR-019 One-Pager]] | Compress one self-contained argument into a page a champion can carry into another room. |
| [[CR-020 Social Media Playbook]] | Keep the narrative stable while adapting each asset to the reading behavior of its platform. |
| [[CR-021 Video and Podcast Craft]] | Use long-form audio or video when sustained explanation and a body of work can build authority. |

Companies can choose only the surfaces relevant to their motion. Match the container's reading conditions to the job, then apply the upstream message and proof disciplines within it.

## Keep the Surface Decisions Separate

[[CR-007 One Message Per Asset]] chooses what a surface may carry. [[CR-017 Visual Hierarchy]] chooses what the eye meets first and how the remaining material follows. The content decision precedes the layout decision, and the finished surface has to honor both.

[[CR-018 Homepage Before-After-Why]] addresses the company's general front door. [[CR-019 One-Pager]] is a forwardable case built for a bounded internal conversation. The homepage can open several routes through the company; the one-pager holds one argument and one next step.

[[OPS-013 Repurposing Pipeline]] owns the workflow that derives assets from a substantial source. [[CR-020 Social Media Playbook]] owns the native form used on each social platform. [[OPS-009 Messaging Cadence]] owns message timing. [[CR-021 Video and Podcast Craft]] governs the medium in which depth and repeated listening can accumulate authority.

## Handoff to Conversion Craft

[[21 Conversion Craft]] inherits surfaces with a clear reading order and a chosen next step. It decides the friction before that step and how the team will test it without manipulating the buyer.
