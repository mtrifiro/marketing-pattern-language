# Conversion Craft

The moment of commitment has to help a serious buyer continue while producing a useful signal for the company. This chapter settles which friction earns its place and what the next step asks of the buyer. It also limits the information a form requires, then sets the decision rule and ethical boundary for testing the result.

The five patterns work from design to evaluation. Friction sets the cost of the step. The invitation defines the relationship the step begins. Form fields make the cost concrete. Experiment design determines what evidence can change the surface. Testing ethics limits which behavior the company is willing to produce.

## From Qualified Step to Ethical Test

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[CR-022 Friction as Qualification]] | Keep friction that produces a meaningful signal at low cost to the intended buyer and remove friction that only wastes effort. |
| [[CR-023 Call to Conversation, Not Conversion]] | Use a low-stakes dialogue as the next step when the buyer is still forming the problem or purchase. |
| [[CR-024 Form as Filter]] | Make each field pay for the conversion cost it creates through information the next step genuinely needs. |
| [[CR-025 Experiment Design]] | State the decision and its single metric, then write the ship-or-kill rule before the test begins. |
| [[CR-026 A-B Testing Ethics]] | Test clearer expression of a true offer and reject variants a fully informed buyer would experience as manipulation. |

The desired conversion is defined before the rate can be interpreted. A lower rate can be useful when the step screens out demand the motion can't serve. A higher rate can be harmful when the variant creates false belief or transfers expensive disqualification to another team.

## Keep the Conversion Decisions Separate

[[CR-022 Friction as Qualification]] sets the intended cost and the ladder of asks. [[CR-024 Form as Filter]] prices that friction one field at a time. A form can be short and still ask for too much commitment; it can be longer and remain appropriate when each field supports a high-trust next step.

[[CR-023 Call to Conversation, Not Conversion]] chooses the kind of next step. It applies where a hard purchase request assumes more certainty than the buyer has developed.

[[CR-010 Message Testing]] owns whether proposed language works with buyers. [[CR-025 Experiment Design]] owns the structure of a test that can settle a decision. [[CR-026 A-B Testing Ethics]] governs what may enter that test before any winning metric appears.

## Handoff to Competitive Craft

[[22 Competitive Craft]] inherits a tested offer and a buyer who is now comparing alternatives. It makes the comparison explicit through competitive surfaces and the visibility of price.
