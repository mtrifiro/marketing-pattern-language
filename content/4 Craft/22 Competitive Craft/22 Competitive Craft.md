# Competitive Craft

A buyer compares the offer with a named rival, an internal build, delay, or continued non-consumption. This chapter settles the dimension that should organize that comparison and the surfaces that carry it. The work stays grounded in the field mapped by [[MS-025 Competitive Geography]], where the most important alternative may have no vendor name.

The sequence moves from strategic choice to buyer-facing execution. One pattern selects the dimension. A public comparison page lets the buyer inspect it. A battle card gives the seller a current answer during a live deal. The pricing page communicates what the seller believes about access and buying complexity before a conversation begins.

## From Axis to Buyer-Facing Evidence

| Pattern | Use in the sequence |
|---|---|
| [[CR-027 Differentiation Axis]] | Choose the dimension on which the company has a structural advantage and buyers can learn to judge the category. |
| [[CR-028 Comparison Page]] | Publish an honest comparison that concedes a real competitor strength and earns trust for the rows you can support. |
| [[CR-029 Battle Card]] | Give sellers one current page built from the questions buyers actually ask about a rival. |
| [[CR-030 Pricing Page Transparency]] | Match the visibility of price to the buying experience and the signal the company intends to send. |

The assets should use current win-loss evidence. Any sign that the axis or its surfaces attract the wrong process should reopen the upstream decision.

## Keep the Competitive Layers Separate

[[MS-022 Positioning as Contrast]] chooses the alternative against which the offer should be understood. [[CR-027 Differentiation Axis]] chooses the dimension on which that judgment runs. A company can contrast itself with an internal build and ask the buyer to judge the choice by deployment time.

[[CR-028 Comparison Page]] is public and buyer-facing. [[CR-029 Battle Card]] is private and seller-facing. They inherit the same axis, while their evidence and use conditions differ.

[[MS-018 Price Creates the Buyer]] owns the amount and the procurement path it calls into the deal. [[CR-030 Pricing Page Transparency]] owns what the visibility of that amount communicates before the buyer speaks with the company.

## Return to the Market

Competitive craft closes the book's altitude sequence by returning observed buyer choice to Market Structure and Operations. [[OPS-002 Win-Loss Discipline]] records which alternative won and why. [[OPS-001 Marketing as Sensemaking]] interprets the recurring evidence. The company can then revise its position and carry the result into motion or message with a traceable reason.
